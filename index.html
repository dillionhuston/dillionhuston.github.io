About

Hello. I'm Dillon, an 18-year-old backend developer from Belfast, Northern Ireland. I started working with technology at 13, writing small scripts that barely ran, but I enjoyed figuring out how systems worked and making them do useful things.

In January 2023, I began freelancing, building small tools, and teaching myself Python properly. Since then I've built APIs, task schedulers, encrypted storage, networking tools, and real-time tracking systems.

My most recent project is RouteReality: a live community-powered tracker for Translink buses. Frustrated by ghost buses and unreliable timetables, I built a system that combines user reports and weighted averages to deliver accurate ETAs across 17,000+ stops. It runs 24/7 on FastAPI and PostgreSQL (v2.0 launched April 2026).

Beyond backend work, I focus on networking, system monitoring, and privacy-focused tools. I am open to junior backend roles, internships, or collaboration, particularly on Python/FastAPI projects or initiatives that improve Network security or research
